## The product

A predictive temperature-monitoring wearable for children. Unlike reactive fever monitors, the bracelet analyzes temperature **trend, slope, and acceleration** to forecast rising spikes early — giving parents time to intervene before dangerous levels are reached. Includes offline alerting (buzzer, vibration, LED) that operates without Wi-Fi or a smartphone.
