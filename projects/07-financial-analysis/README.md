## Scope

Full financial analysis of a listed Jordanian company across a 5-year window (2017–2021), covering all three primary financial statements plus a comprehensive ratio study and investment recommendations.

## Part I — Statement analysis

### Balance sheet (2017–2021)

Horizontal and vertical analysis of:
- **Total Assets** — grew from 72.6 M → 86.1 M JD (business expansion signal)
- **Total Liabilities** — grew from 49.7 M → 67.2 M JD (debt-financed growth)
- **Shareholders' Equity** — dropped from 22.2 M → 18.9 M JD (declining self-financing)

### Income statement

Operating revenues, operating expenses, gross profit, and net income tracked year-over-year with explanations of each shift (pricing, cost efficiency, technology adoption, market expansion).

- **Operating revenue growth:** 36.04 M → 41.11 M JD
- **Operating expense reduction:** 37.04 M → 34.75 M JD
- **Gross profit turnaround:** −1.24 M → +6.34 M JD
- **Net income improvement:** 0.21 M → 2.15 M JD

### Cash flow statement

Vertical and horizontal analysis of operating, investing, and financing activities.

## Part II — Ratio analysis (15+ ratios)

Computed and interpreted year-over-year:

| Category | Ratios |
|----------|--------|
| **Leverage** | Debt Ratio, Times Interest Earned |
| **Liquidity** | Current, Quick, Liquidity (cash) |
| **Efficiency** | Inventory Turnover, DSO, DSI, Total Asset Turnover |
| **Profitability** | Gross Margin, Net Margin, ROA, ROE |
| **Market** | EPS, P/E, Book Value |

**Selected findings:**
- **Debt Ratio** climbed 68.4% → 79.2% (2017 → 2020) — high leverage
- **Current Ratio** dropped below 1.0 in 2019–2020 — short-term liquidity stress
- **P/E = 100** in 2017 — very high market valuation relative to earnings

## Part III — Recommendations

Structured recommendations covering:
1. **Profitability enhancement** — revenue growth, pricing optimization, operating-expense reduction, depreciation strategy
2. **Cash flow management** — receivables acceleration, payables delay, inventory control, JIT
3. **Strategic actions** — high-margin product focus, growth investments, quarterly performance review, capital structure optimization
4. **Technology adoption** — cash-flow management software, ERP integration
5. **Risk management** — income stream diversification, hedging

Working capital estimation included accounts receivable, finished goods inventory, raw material inventory, and accounts payable calculations.
