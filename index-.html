About

I'm an Industrial Engineering student in my third year at Al-Hussein Technical University (HTU) in Jordan, focused on the intersection of production systems, optimization, and data analysis.

My coursework and research have taken me across the discipline — from discrete-event simulation of automotive assembly lines to integer linear programming for last-mile drone logistics, from full value-stream mapping of a food manufacturing line to financial-ratio analysis of a listed company. What ties it together: every project ends with numbers that actually mean something for a real decision.

I'm looking for internships and opportunities where I can apply these methods to operational problems that matter — with organizations that value quantitative rigor and a genuine engineering mindset.
